# Fix Root Causes

When to apply: When debugging a reported failure.

Reproduce the symptom, ask why repeatedly, instrument rather than guess, and inspect sibling occurrences of the same pattern. A failure after restart warrants checking persisted state.

Boundary: Do not treat every defensive check as a cover-up. Use reproduction evidence to identify the root cause.
